## Inter

Two official Inter 4.1 variable WOFF2 files are retrieved at build time from the
upstream `v4.1` Git tag. `InterVariable.woff2` supplies normal weights 400, 500,
and 600; `InterVariable-Italic.woff2` supplies italic weight 400. Their exact
URLs and SHA-256 checksums are pinned in `assets/fonts/inter-4.1.json`. Inter is
copyright The Inter Project Authors and is licensed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1. See `licenses/INTER.txt`.

Source: <https://github.com/rsms/inter/releases/tag/v4.1>
